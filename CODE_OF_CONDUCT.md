# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes contributors of any background, belief, or level of
experience. The standard is simple, and it is the one the project is named
after:

> Love your neighbour as yourself. — Mark 12:31

In practice, that means:

- **Be patient.** People arrive with different amounts of context. A
  question that seems obvious to you may not be obvious to the person
  asking, and answering it well costs little.
- **Be honest and kind at the same time.** Say what you actually think
  about the code, clearly. Direct technical criticism is welcome and
  useful; contempt for the person who wrote it is not.
- **Critique the work, not the author.** "This has a race condition
  because…" is helpful. "Did you even test this?" is not.
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements are misunderstandings, and
  most mistakes are honest ones.
- **Give credit.** Acknowledge the work and ideas of others.

This is a project about the Bible, and contributors will hold a range of
religious views — including none. Discussion of the text, of translations,
and of their provenance is on topic and welcome. Proselytising at other
contributors, or disparaging their beliefs or lack of them, is not.

This is simply the wrong place for it. People came here to work on a
guessing game, and an issue tracker is a poor setting for a conversation
that deserves a better one. That is a point about the venue, not about the
value of anyone's convictions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including about religion.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberate bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, commit messages,
code comments — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to **<sukkerfrit@gmail.com>**. Reports will be
handled discreetly, and the reporter's identity will not be shared without
their consent.

This is a small project maintained by one person, so please be patient with
response times. That person is also the one you would be reporting to; if
that is a problem for the report you need to make, say so, and it will be
taken seriously rather than defended against.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily
block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.
Where a decision is made, the reasoning will be explained to the person
affected — an unexplained ban teaches nobody anything.

Corrections will be made in proportion to the problem. A first
thoughtless comment is not treated like a sustained pattern.
